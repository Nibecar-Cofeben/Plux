I got tired of travel spreadsheets and $50/yr paywalls, so I built an open-source trip planner

Nibecar Cofeben
Nov 4, 2026 · 4 min read
Read on DEV.to

During a trip across Italy, I hit the exact same wall that thousands of travelers face: planning was complete chaos. I was juggling messy Excel spreadsheets, copying and pasting recommendations from AI chat tabs, keeping 30 browser tabs open, and trying to figure out who paid for what in group chats.

When I looked at existing tools like Wanderlog, I hit immediate paywalls for essential features like route optimization and AI assistance. Other apps felt like rigid corporate tools from 2008 or forced every single person in the group to download an app and create an account just to see the itinerary.

I wanted something lightweight, fast, completely open, and without commercial friction. So I built Plux.

What is Plux?

Plux is an open-source, 100% free Progressive Web App (PWA) designed to organize group and solo trips in one unified place without subscriptions, ads, or paywalls.

Core Features

Modular Day-by-Day Itinerary

Visual scheduling organized by stops, activities, and transport, with real-time route visualization.

Real-Time Group Collaboration

Edit the trip simultaneously with travel companions without version conflicts.

Zero Forced Registration

You can start planning or share a trip immediately without forcing everyone in the group to register or create passwords.

Integrated Budget & Multi-Currency

Track expenses, view currency conversions, and balance group spending directly inside the trip workspace.

Built-in Travel Utilities

Interactive packing checklists, live weather forecasts, currency converters, and integrated notes.

Offline-First & PDF Export

Instant structured PDF export and client-side persistence so you can access your plans without internet connectivity.

Contextual AI Assistant

Built-in intelligent assistant to help optimize stops, suggest schedules, and resolve destination logistics without paywalls.

The Tech & Philosophy Behind It

Instead of bloating the project with heavy dependencies, Plux was built with an offline-first and zero-friction philosophy:

  • Vanilla JavaScript & Modern Semantic CSS Prioritizing raw browser performance, fast load times on mobile, and low memory consumption.
  • PWA Architecture Works smoothly across desktop and mobile browsers, installable directly without going through app store hurdles.
  • Privacy & Freedom 100% open source, ad-free, and community-driven.
